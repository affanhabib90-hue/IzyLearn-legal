Privacy Policy

IzyLearn — Free Offline English for Toddlers · Bundle ID com.izylearn.izylearn · Version 2.0.0 · Last updated September 2026

Overview

IzyLearn is a free, offline-first English learning app for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2–6), designed to be used by young children together with a parent or guardian. There are no accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. IzyLearn never asks for a name, email address or account, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs (no Firebase, Google Analytics or similar services).

Data stored on your device

Only the following is kept in local device storage so the app can remember progress while working fully offline:

  • The child’s selected age plan
  • Learned letters, numbers and words
  • Completed activities, stars and session statistics for the parent dashboard

This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the app or use Parents → Reset progress.

Internet use

IzyLearn does not use the internet at all. Every letter, word, song and story ships inside the app; there is nothing to download and no network request is ever made. The Android build does not even request the INTERNET permission.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions: no microphone, camera, contacts, location, SMS/call or fitness APIs are used.

Children’s privacy

IzyLearn is made for families and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parent settings and progress reports are protected by a parental gate (a simple arithmetic question plus a confirmation button) that a child cannot pass on their own.

AI-generated content

The illustrations, spoken audio, music and narration in this app were produced with the assistance of artificial intelligence. All content was reviewed and tested manually before release, but AI-generated material may still contain occasional imperfections.

Contact & changes

Questions about privacy: customerservicahapps@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page and inside the app (Parents → Privacy) with a new “last updated” date.
