Privacy Policy

IzyLearn
Bundle ID: com.babygrowlearn.app
Version 1.0.15+19 | Effective Date: August 19, 2026
For Parents: IzyLearn is designed for toddlers and young children. We take privacy seriously and have built this app to protect your child's information.

Information We Collect

Learning data stays on your device. IzyLearn does not create cloud accounts for children and does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs:

  • Learning Progress: Quiz scores, achievements, stickers, and screen time data are stored locally on your device only
  • On-device activity logs: Session length and words practiced for the parent dashboard stay on the device (not Google Analytics, Firebase, or similar)
  • Voice Recordings: Parent-recorded pronunciations for learning words are saved locally
  • Child Profiles: Names, ages, and preferences are stored on-device in local storage
  • Parent Settings: App lock PIN, session limits, and bedtime schedules remain on your device
  • Family Photos: Optional parent-uploaded photos for family word learning stay on the device
  • Crash log: A short local error log may be kept on the device. It is sent off-device only if a given store binary was built with an optional crash webhook (the Kids-safe store build should not enable this)

We do not operate a server that receives your child’s learning progress.

Network Activity

In the standard Kids-safe App Store build, Animals, Weather, and Flags use built-in offline practice packs and do not contact third-party servers.

Optional live network APIs are disabled by default so the child experience does not send device or network information to third parties.

Permissions We Request

The app may request these permissions for specific features:

  • Microphone: Parent voice recordings (custom word pronunciations) and optional Say It practice. Recordings you create stay on the device.
  • Speech Recognition: Optional Say It practice (ages 5–6). Processing uses the device’s speech services provided by the operating system.
  • Photo Library: Parents may add family photos behind the parent gate. Photos stay on the device.
  • Location: Not required. Weather words use an offline practice pack.

Data Storage

  • All learning progress, profiles, and settings are stored in local device storage using Flutter's SharedPreferences and Hive databases
  • No child learning progress is synchronized to cloud services or external servers
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data on that device

Children's Privacy

IzyLearn is designed for children ages 1–6 under parent or caregiver supervision. The Kids-safe store build is built around these practices (this describes the product; it is not a legal certification of COPPA or other laws):

  • We do not require account creation or login
  • There is no social sharing or communication features
  • The app contains no advertisements or in-app purchases
  • No third-party advertising or cross-app analytics SDKs
  • Parent settings are protected by a parental gate (typed math puzzle or parent PIN)
  • Child learning progress stays on the device

No Advertising or Tracking

  • This app contains zero advertisements
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • On-device activity logs for parents stay on the device
  • No tracking cookies or device fingerprinting
  • No behavioral profiling for advertising

Data Security

  • All app data is stored locally using industry-standard Flutter storage mechanisms
  • The Parent Settings area is protected by a parental gate to prevent children from accessing settings
  • We do not have access to your device data or the ability to retrieve it remotely

Your Parental Controls

Parents have full control over app features:

  • Set daily screen time limits
  • Configure bedtime mode to lock the app during sleep hours
  • Set or change a parent PIN
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling
  • Reset profiles and progress from Parent Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The version and effective date at the top of this page show the current policy. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

International Users

IzyLearn is available worldwide. Child learning progress stays on the device. Optional operating-system speech services may process audio according to the device’s privacy settings.

App Permissions Summary

Android Permissions:
  • RECORD_AUDIO — parent voice recordings and pronunciation practice
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / storage (older Android) — optional parent family photos
  • INTERNET may appear in the manifest for OS/TTS tooling; Kids-safe child explore packs do not call third-party content APIs
  • Location is not requested
iOS Permissions:
  • Microphone — voice recordings and speech practice
  • Speech Recognition — optional Say It practice
  • Photo Library — optional parent family photos
  • Location is not requested in the Kids-safe App Store build
