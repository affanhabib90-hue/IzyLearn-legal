Quick help

  • Parent settings: On the Today tab, press and hold the gear icon, then solve the math puzzle (or enter your parent PIN).
  • Change age or language: Parent → Profile & Language.
  • Screen time / bedtime: Parent → Time & Safety.
  • Family photos / voice: Parent hub (behind the gate).
  • Delete data: Uninstall the app, or reset from Parent Settings where available.

Privacy & Kids safety

IzyLearn has no ads and no in-app purchases. Learning progress stays on your device. Explore packs (Animals, Weather, Flags) use offline content in the App Store Kids-safe build.

App Store review tip

If you are an Apple reviewer: long-press the gear on Today to open the parental gate. Default PIN cannot unlock until a parent sets a custom PIN — use the typed math puzzle.
