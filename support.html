Email customerservicahapps@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. Please include the device model and the IzyLearn version from the store listing (current release: 2.0.0).

Quick answers

How do I reset progress? Open Parents (lock icon) → answer the math check → Continue → Reset progress.

Why doesn’t the app need the internet? Every letter, word, song and story ships inside the app. IzyLearn is fully offline by design.

How do I delete all data? Uninstall the app — nothing is stored anywhere else.

Is there a paid version? No. IzyLearn is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Why does the sound not play? Check that the device volume and the app’s speaker button are on, and mute is off (tablets in silent mode play no audio).
